# trial-kit 🧰

**Intent.** Make the trial startable by someone who is not the person who
built moot. Everything that does not depend on the trial having happened.

Split out of [[board-trial]] because the run itself needs two fanta gremlins,
a human, and real work over real elapsed time — none of which can be
manufactured. A staged conversation proves nothing, so the honest split is
"make it ready" and "run it".

## Deliver

- `setup.md` — the exact commands to stand up one shared room and wire three
  participants to it, including the choice of filesystem vs sync vs git and
  why that choice changes the merge story under test.
- `observations.md` — the watch-for list as a sheet to fill in during the
  trial, so findings are recorded as they happen rather than reconstructed
  afterwards.

## Tie-off

A person with two gremlin hosts can start the trial from `setup.md` without
reading any other document, and has somewhere to write down what they see.
